## Two separate questions
1. **Patentability:** Is a specific implemented improvement new, useful, non-obvious and adequately described? An established device plus routine ESP32 wireless control is unlikely to justify a broad claim without a genuinely new technical result.
2. **Freedom to operate:** Does manufacturing, importing, selling or using our exact implementation read on an enforceable patent claim in each market? Having our own patent would not by itself give permission to practice it.

## Before selling
- Search Google Patents, USPTO Patent Center / Patent Public Search, Espacenet, and WIPO Patentscope with variations of 'balloon burst', 'thermal conductor', 'remote', 'cue', 'puncture', 'heating wire', 'actuation'.
- Obtain official prosecution/maintenance/assignment status, check claim amendments and related family members.
- Make a proposed bill of materials, schematic, attachment cross-sections, firmware workflow and full use-case; commission professional claim charts for active families.
- Search third-party trademarks and trade dress separately.
- If a potentially novel feature emerges, consider invention disclosure and counsel on provisional timing **before publicizing specifics**.
- Recheck landscape before release because new applications publish and rights/status change.
