## Business implications
- Existing products and patents rule out treating 'remote pop a balloon' or 'heat a wire to pop a balloon' as novel in themselves.
- Market validation needs current sell-through, warranty/reliability information, and customer interviews. Listing prices alone do not establish addressable market.
- Candidate differentiators to **investigate** (not assertions of novelty): paired secure individual node control; measurable link quality; safe fault states; efficient installations; serviceable power; clearly documented open ecosystem; repeatable production QC.
- Do not copy competitor PCB layout, firmware, branding, mechanical details or documentation.
